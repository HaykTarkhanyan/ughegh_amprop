The idea in one paragraph

An imagined plate of tolma supplies the local kitchen anchor and the opening puzzle: why does the plate turn? The explanation starts with a changing electric field acting on interacting water molecules and transferring energy into heat; water is important but not the only absorber. The single extension zooms out to the cavity: reflections produce stronger and weaker field regions, and the turntable moves food through them. The viewer tracks a marked tolma piece through the pattern. The doubt beat asks whether this guarantees equal temperatures; it improves averaging but does not guarantee uniform heating. No water-resonance, freely spinning molecules, candy-bar anecdote, or kitchen light-speed measurement is introduced.

Facts (checked)

Evidence base: _knowledge/short_videos/facts_codex_7_physics_everyday_2026-11-28.md, P4. All used sheet claims are high confidence. Vollmer's article and Baird's university explanation were also read online during drafting. The local food connection is an additional web check, explicitly separated from the physical example.

  • Frequency (field): most conventional domestic ovens nominally operate at 2.45 GHz. This is neither the mains frequency nor a special resonant frequency of water. CCOHS, Microwave Ovens, revised 2024, Vollmer, 2004, p. 74, Baird, 2014. The nominal-frequency statement is the only numerical physics value displayed or voiced.
  • Dielectric heating (water, heat): the changing field acts on molecular dipoles. Neighbour interactions and lag cause energy dissipation; the animation must not equate field oscillations with complete molecular rotations. The plain charge-distribution explanation replaces an unexplained dipole label. Vollmer, pp. 78-79, Baird.
  • Other absorbers (heat): dissolved ions and other constituents can contribute; the draft does not say only water heats. Vollmer, p. 79.
  • Standing patterns and turntable (hook, hot_spots, turntable): wall reflections produce spatially uneven fields. A turntable moves food through different regions and improves averaging. Some designs instead use a mode stirrer, so the opening is explicitly this imagined turntable-equipped oven, not a universal design claim. Vollmer, p. 77.
  • Limits of the simplified image (doubt): real cavity fields and the food's loading and dielectric properties are more complex. Rotation does not guarantee an even temperature. The animation is qualitative, without a supposedly universal hot-spot spacing or an invented measured heat map. Vollmer, pp. 77-79, Baird, load-dependent field/frequency discussion.
  • Additional local anchor, verified on the web (hook, turntable, closing): the Tourism Committee's official guide identifies tolma as a traditional Armenian dish. Armenia Travel, Armenian Cuisine, opened 2026-11-28. The reheating scene begins «Պատկերացրո՛ւ»: it is an illustrative application of the mechanism, not an experiment on tolma or a claim about its cooking time, temperature, composition percentages, or origin.
  • Medium-or-lower claims used: none. The medium-confidence historical selection story, candy anecdote and disputed commercial-launch dates are omitted. No additional biography or invention-history extension is used.
Animation plan (showcase)
  • One continuous showcase, hot_spots into turntable: frame 0 of hot_spots contains the cavity and reflected wave arrows. Build a simplified standing-field section, distinguishing strong and weak oscillation regions. Positive and negative field antinodes both heat; field sign is not a hot/cold distinction. Move out to the plate and follow a marked tolma piece along a non-central circular path through the qualitative pattern. Keep «Պարզեցված պատկեր» visible. The 2 s field pause is for comparing regions; the longest pause, 4 s, continues the same showcase so the viewer can follow the marked piece. Do not draw concentric uniform rings that rotation cannot move the marked piece across, or imply all paths sample identical fields.
  • Supporting beats: hook has the tolma and rotating plate in frame 0, labelled «նկարազարդում». field slows the arrow reversals explicitly; the animation speed is not 2.45 GHz. water starts with neighbouring dipoles and shows lag and interactions; heat adds ion motion and a qualitative heat tint, not free-propeller molecules.
  • Doubt and closing: retain a visibly uneven tint after rotation, with no asserted temperature scale. Allow 2 s to inspect it. Return to the same imaginary plate, say the closing screen line, and fade the field overlay. Keep the illustration label. No summary card. Total marked pauses: 10 s; longest: 4 s on the showcase.
  • No chocolate experiment, fixed centimetre spacing, measured cooking time, inside-out claim or historical laboratory scene. Every beat begins on a relevant object rather than a title-only frame.
