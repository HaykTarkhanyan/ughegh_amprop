The idea in one paragraph

On this map Greenland is nearly Africa's size; Africa is really 14 times larger. Drag Greenland to the equator and it shrinks below Algeria; lift Armenia to Greenland's latitude and it swells about six times. A sphere cannot be flattened without distortion (try an orange peel; Gauss proved there is no way around it). So why draw the world like this? Gerardus Mercator, a 16th-century mapmaker, made his map for sailors: it keeps angles, so a straight line is a constant compass course, which forces equal stretching north-south and east-west. The stretch grows towards the poles and the pole itself is infinitely far, so the map is cut near 85 degrees. Equal-area maps fix sizes and wreck shapes; every map sacrifices something. Try it: zoom all the way out in Google Maps on a computer and the map becomes a globe. The takeaway: a map does not lie, it answers one question, and Mercator's was how not to get lost at sea.

Facts (checked)
  • Areas: Greenland about 2.17 million km², Africa about 30.4 million km² (14.0 times); Algeria 2.38 million km², so Greenland is smaller than Algeria (NPR 2026, Mental Floss, data.europa.eu guide).
  • Mercator's stretch: linear scale sec φ, area scale sec² φ (standard). Computed here: x1.71 at Armenia's 40.2°N, x4.0 at 60°, x10.5 at 72°N (mid-Greenland), so Armenia moved to 72°N would look 10.47 / 1.71 = 6.1 times bigger than on today's Mercator map ("about six"). Web Mercator is cut at about 85.05°.
  • Why Mercator: a conformal projection (glossary: «կոնֆորմ արտապատկերում»), made in 1569 for navigation; rhumb lines (constant compass bearing) are straight. Standard; the year is on the source line, not in the voice.
  • Gauss: Theorema Egregium (1827): a sphere's curvature cannot be kept on a plane, so no flat map is free of distortion.
  • Google Maps: desktop switched to a 3D globe when zoomed out, announced 2 August 2018; Google's own example was Greenland no longer looking as large as Africa; mobile kept the flat map (Engadget, TechCrunch, TechRadar). The script says «համակարգչում».
  • Left out: the Boston schools switching to Gall-Peters (2017) was not confirmed in the search.
