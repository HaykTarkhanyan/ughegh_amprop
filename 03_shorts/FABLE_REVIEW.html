0. How to read this, and the length check
  • Every item that needs a decision has an ID, F1 to F152, one sequence for the whole file. Answer by ID.
  • Armenian items are questions, not corrections. [heard] = structural, about following it by ear (sentence length, ambiguity, number density): fairly reliable. [ear] = "sounds off to me": the style guide measured this kind of model judgement as right 1 time in 9, so treat those as low-confidence.
  • "Gloss" = an English version of a proposed line. The Armenian wording is Hayk's.
  • Beat times are computed from character counts, not from audio.

The "numbers spoken" columns. The 870 characters a minute were measured on the pilot, whose text has no digits at all. These scripts write numbers as digits: «1986» counts as 4 characters but is spoken as «հազար ինը հարյուր ութսունվեց» (27 characters). I spelled out every number and recounted. This is an estimate, not a measurement: it assumes the voice reads a spelled-out number at its normal rate, and it does not adjust Latin letters (QWERTY, Z).

What the numbers say

  1. qwerty is over. Raw count: 167 s of speech, already past the 165 s cap. With its 7 years read aloud: about 180 s of speech, 3:02 in total, past the hard limit. It must lose two beats: 10 (path) and 5 (remington), and beat 9 must shrink (see F80, F81).
  2. regression and crypto_paint are at the cap once numbers are spoken (165 s of speech, no margin). Each loses one beat: regression beat 10 (galton, F31), crypto_paint beat 12 (history, F57).
  3. bayes_test, simpson, waiting are inside the limit by about 5 s. No forced cut, but I recommend one anyway for editorial reasons: bayes beat 4, simpson beat 11 (and 9), waiting beat 5. kids_tv (added) is inside by about 8 s; it has only four numbers written as digits, so the two estimates are close. Same advice: beat 9 (guidelines, F139).
  4. friendship, petrov, own_voice, yawning are fine.
  5. No script leaves room for silence. build_timeline.py joins beats with a fixed 0.12 s gap, and every script is sized to fill its time with speech. The showcases need silent seconds (the paint swirl, the alarm, the network drawing itself). Budget a showcase at about 150 s of speech (about 2,175 spoken characters) so 10-15 s are left for the animation.
  6. Check the estimate with one TTS call (not done here, it is a paid API): voice simpson beat 4 (141 characters, 3 three-digit numbers). If it comes back at about 14 s and not 9.7 s, the spelled-out count is the right one for the whole batch.
  7. Caption timing. build_timeline.py gives each word time in proportion to its letter count, so «1986» gets the time of a 4-letter word. Expect the word-by-word captions to slip around numbers, most in bayes_test (about 40 number mentions by my rough count, against 3 in the pilot) and simpson. check_sync.py should show it.
  8. Measured since (note added with kids_tv). After the first version of this review two beats were voiced (tts_ledger.csv): simpson beat 4 came back at 11.8 s (raw estimate 9.7 s, spelled-out estimate 14.0 s) and the petrov hook at 12.6 s (10.4 s and 12.5 s). So the truth lies between the two columns of the table, and plan_script.py now uses their midpoint. On the midpoint qwerty is about 174 s of speech, still over the cap, about 2:56 in total; regression and crypto_paint are about 162 s, 3 s under the cap and not on it. The cuts I recommend stand, since they were editorial as much as arithmetic, but for regression and crypto_paint "must lose a beat" becomes "should". build_timeline.py now weights a number by its spoken length, so item 7 is dealt with. The table keeps the uncalibrated method so that all eleven rows are comparable.
1. bayes_test

14 beats, 2,182 characters, about 2:33 (about 2:42 with numbers spoken).

Hook: strong. Personal, one number, one question, under 8 s, and the situation is clear after 4 s. Keep.

Core idea. Clear and repeatable up to beat 8 (the first 67 s): "a positive on a 99% test for a rare disease can be a coin flip, because the healthy outnumber the sick". After that the script adds four more number scenarios (9%, doctors 21% and 87%, retest 99%, symptoms 92%) and a formula. Where a viewer gets lost:

  • "99" ends up with three meanings: the test's accuracy, the wrong intuitive answer, and in beat 11 the probability after a second test. "About 99 percent" as the good news at 1:55, after a minute of "not 99", is confusing by ear.
  • About 40 number mentions in 2.5 minutes.

Pacing.

  • F1. Cut beat 4 (meaning). It leaves the crowd for a side panel and comes back, and it says what beat 6 says again ("1 of 100 healthy is flagged" / "1 percent of them, about ten"). Fold one clause into beat 5. Saves about 9 s and removes two tiny 10 x 10 blocks squeezed next to a 1,000-dot grid.
  • F2. Merge beat 9 (rarer) and beat 12 (symptoms) into one "same test, different rarity" beat: 1 in 1,000 gives 9%, 1 in 100 gives 50%, 1 in 10 (you have symptoms) gives 92%. They are one idea seen from two sides, and it is exactly what the takeaway asks. Saves about 12 s and a grid reset.
  • F3. Move beat 10 (doctors) to right after beat 8 and tie it to the video itself (gloss: "after one hour of counting people and not percentages, 87 percent got it right. You just did the same in one minute."). The script has the material for this line and does not use it.
  • F4. Beat 11 (retest) is the longest beat (23 s). Keep three things: repeat the test by another method; a healthy person almost never tests positive twice; that is why a positive HIV test is always confirmed. Drop the "about 99 percent" figure (the collision above).
  • Rushed: beat 7 (reveal) has four sentences in 11 s while 20 dots fly out and regroup. It needs about 1.5 s without voice between «Քսան մարդ» and the 50%.
  • F5. Beat 13 (formula): 8 s for notation that almost nobody reads on a phone in 4 s. It was decided (Q13), so keep it, but draw it as counted dots first: sick positives over all positives = 10 / 20, with P(D | +) small under it.

Ending. Lands. A question the viewer can repeat, it echoes the hook's "99 percent accurate", and the frame loops. It is the same sentence frame as four other scripts (section 11).

Visual ideas.

  • F6. Give the viewer a dot. Mark one dot «դու» in the hook; at the reveal the 20 positives line up and the question on screen is "which of these 20 are you?": 10 red, 10 grey. It ties the hook's "your result" to the 50% better than a fraction does.
  • F7. Do F2 as one continuous shot: a rarity slider moves from 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10, red dots appear in the same grid, and a bar "of the positives, really sick" slides 9% -> 50% -> 92%. No resets, no new grids.

Armenian (questions).

  • F8 [heard] «Թեստի փոքր՝ մեկ տոկոս սխալը կիրառվում է հսկայական խմբի վրա»: the inserted «փոքր՝ մեկ տոկոս» is hard to parse by ear and «կիրառվում է ... վրա» is written language. Two short sentences (the error is small; the group is huge)?
  • F9 [heard] «կրծքագեղձի քաղցկեղի սկրինինգի մասին»: three genitives in a row plus a loanword (U-b). Does the viewer need the topic of the doctors' question at all?
  • F10 [heard] «երբ թվերը բացատրել են մարդկանց քանակով» can be heard as "explained by the number of people". Reuse beat 2's own phrase («ոչ թե տոկոսներով, այլ մարդկանցով»)?
  • F11 Agreement: «Ճիշտ է պատասխանել միայն ամեն հինգերորդը» (singular), then «ճիշտ են պատասխանել 87 տոկոսը» (plural). Is the plural intended?
  • F12 [ear] «Ո՞րն է հավանականությունը, որ ...» sounds like an exercise; «Մեծ մասը կասի ... Բժիշկների մեծ մասը նույնպես» has «մեծ մասը» with no noun, then again. Also «դրական պատասխան» comes 9 times: could some be just «դրական»?
  • F13 TTS: check how the voice reads «ՄԻԱՎ-ի» and «99-ին».

Verdict: small fixes. The first minute is the best explanation in the batch. The second minute is four more lessons; F1-F4 turn it into two.

2. simpson

13 beats, 2,101 characters, about 2:27 (about 2:42 with numbers spoken; this script gains the most, 15 numbers).

Hook: ok. The first sentence («Երկու բուժում երիկամի քարերի համար») is a label with no tension, and the question arrives at about 9 s. The real hook is beat 2.

  • F14. Open on the choice and the twist together (gloss: "78 percent or 83 percent: which treatment do you pick? Careful: the 78 one is better for every group of patients."). Both numbers are on screen in frame 0 already.

Core idea. "The better treatment looked worse because it got the harder patients": clear, repeatable, and beat 8 shows it well. Where a viewer gets lost:

  • F15. Three naming systems for the same two things: «առաջին / երկրորդ» (hook), «Ա / Բ» (screen), «բաց վիրահատություն» against «երկրորդ մեթոդ» (beats 4-9). One treatment has a name, the other is "the second method". Pick one pair and use it in the voice and on screen from the first beat.
  • Beats 6-7: four percentages in about 13 s.
  • Beat 11 (covid): one 173-character sentence that cannot be understood until its last clause.
  • Beat 12: the exception ("if the split is a result of the treatment, trust the total") has no example and comes right after the viewer learned the opposite.

Pacing.

  • F16. Beats 3-4 (about 0:19 to 0:40, later with the numbers read aloud) restate the hook: 78 and 83 become 273 of 350 and 289 of 350. The viewer was promised the split at 0:10 and waits half a minute for it. Merge 3 and 4 into one beat and show 273 and 289 on screen without speaking them.
  • F17. Cut beat 9 (weighted) as narration. Its second sentence repeats beat 8; its first is a definition. If the term matters, put «կշռավորված միջին» on screen under beat 8.
  • F18. Cut beat 11 (covid) (U-g). It is the weakest of the three examples: the hardest sentence, a sensitive topic, and its bars are schematic drawings of two real countries, which viewers will read as data. That sits badly with the honesty rule. Berkeley alone proves "it happens elsewhere".
  • F19. Cut the second sentence of beat 12 (U-h). The first sentence already carries the condition ("something that existed before the treatment"), so honesty survives; an exception with no example only blurs the takeaway.
  • Rushed: beat 5 (split) is 2.3 s of speech for the most important animation of the video. Give it 2-3 s without voice.
  • With F16-F19 the script is about 2:00-2:05, which leaves room for that pause.

Ending. Fine: a repeatable question, and the frame returns to the two cards. Same frame as bayes_test.

Visual ideas.

  • F20. Show stone size as dot size (large stone = large dot) from the split onward, and keep colour for the outcome only. The plan recolours the same dots from outcome (white / red) to size (dark / light orange) in beat 8, so the meaning of colour changes in the middle of the video.
  • F21. Replace the weighted-sum card (93% x 87 + 73% x 263 -> 78%, unreadable on a phone) with one slider per treatment: a marker sits between that treatment's two group rates and is pulled towards the group with more dots. A's marker lands near 73, B's near 87. That is the weighted average with no term and no formula.

Armenian (questions).

  • F22 «դիմում այն ֆակուլտետներ, որտեղ ընդունվում էին քչերը»: after «այն», should the noun be definite («այն ֆակուլտետները, որտեղ ...»)?
  • F23 [heard] Beat 10: 44, 35, six, four in 20 s. Drop "of the six largest ... in four" and say "in most of the large departments"?
  • F24 [heard] If covid stays: split the sentence in two (higher overall; lower in every age group).
  • F25 [ear] «73 տոկոսն ընդդեմ 69-ի» («ընդդեմ» in speech?); «Իսկ հիմա՝» as the twist opener (does it carry "but wait"?); «ընդհանուր թիվը վատ է երևում».

Verdict: small fixes, mostly cuts.

3. regression

14 beats, 2,294 characters, about 2:41 (about 2:47 with numbers spoken: 165 s of speech, at the cap). One beat must go.

Hook: strong. Everyday, parallel, the first sentence takes 3 s, and it ends on a provocative question.

  • F26 [ear] The third sentence is the bookish one («նախատինքն ավելի արդյունավե՞տ է, քան գովասանքը»). A blunter question would hit harder (gloss: "So should we shout more?").

Core idea. Clear: "after an extreme result the next one is usually more average, whatever you do". Risks:

  • Seven settings in under three minutes: flights, exams, flights again, heights, a doctor, a coach, two groups.
  • F27. Three words for luck in two adjacent beats («բախտ», «հաջողություն», «պատահականություն») and three names for the idea («միջինին վերադարձ», «հետ գնալ դեպի միջինը», «ավելի միջին արդյունք»; U-i). By ear, one concept needs one word, repeated.
  • Beat 8 (reverse) is the most original proof and the most abstract.

Pacing.

  • F28. Beat 2 (kahneman, 21-23 s) repeats the hook in the instructor's mouth: the same observation is made at 0:03 and again at 0:20-0:30. Cut it to one sentence (gloss: "An Israeli flight instructor said exactly this to the psychologist Daniel Kahneman."), or open the video with the instructor's own line and drop the generic hook. Saves about 12 s.
  • F29. Beat 9 (praise): its first three sentences restate what the animation just showed. Keep the first and the last one («թվերը մեզ «պատժում» են բարի լինելու համար ...»), which is the line people will quote.
  • F30. Three closing beats in a row: check (12 s), card (7 s), takeaway (7 s). The takeaway's question is the check beat in question form, and the card repeats beat 4. Keep check and takeaway; show the card on screen under the takeaway with no narration of its own.
  • F31. Cut beat 10 (galton, 19 s). A history detour at 1:45 that adds a name and a year. The term can sit on screen. This is the beat that must go.
  • Beat 11 (everyday): keep. The sacked coach is the example viewers will retell.
  • Rushed: beat 8 needs a 2 s hold after «Բախտը չգիտի, թե որն է առաջինը, որը՝ երկրորդը».
  • F32 (optional). Simulate 1,000 cadets' flights and not 1,000 students' exams: one setting fewer, and beat 9 follows without a jump. Exams are more familiar to viewers, so this is Hayk's call.

Ending. Loops to the hook. «առանց դրանց» ("without them") is vague by ear.

  • F33. A blunter close would match the hook (gloss: "Don't shout. The next try would have been better anyway.").

Visual ideas.

  • F34. Two axes, not one histogram that rearranges: test 1 on top, test 2 below, one line per highlighted student. The best 50 fan in towards the middle. For the reverse beat keep the same picture and highlight from the bottom axis upward: the mirror-image funnel shows "it works backwards" at a glance, where an animation played in reverse reads as "undo".
  • F35. Show luck on the dots: when the best 50 light up, each gets a small arrow for its luck on that test, and almost all point up. On test 2 the arrows are drawn again at random. This shows why they fall; the current plan only says it.

Armenian (questions).

  • F36 The negative side has four names: «նախատել» (hook), «գոռալ» (instructor), «պատիժ» (Kahneman), «խիստ լինել» (beat 9). One word for the series of beats? (U-j)
  • F37 [heard] «որոնց սովորաբար հաջորդում են ավելի միջինները ... ավելի լավերը ... ամենավատերից հետո»: stacked adjectives used as nouns, and «ավելի միջին» ("more average") in particular.
  • F38 «նույն քննությունը գրում է երկու անգամ»: literally the same exam, so memory would help? «երկու նման քննություն»?
  • F39 [ear] «լավ հմտություն է՝ գումարած հաջողություն»; «գործը բախտինն էր» (idiomatic, or forced?); «Կարճահասակներինը՝ կարճ» if Galton stays.
  • F40 TTS: «1960-ականներին».

Verdict: small fixes (F28, F30, F31).

4. waiting

12 beats, 2,301 characters, about 2:41.

Hook: weak. «Յոթ րոպե կանգնած սպասելն ավելի երկար է թվում, քան վեց րոպե քայլելը»: seven minutes is longer than six, so the claim sounds like a truism, and the on-screen «7 րոպե > 6 րոպե?» is simply true (the label is quoted as the plan has it, with a Latin question mark). The Houston contrast is 1 + 7 against 6 + 2, the same total. The best hook in the script is buried in beat 9: «Իսկ ինչո՞ւ է մյուս հերթը միշտ ավելի արագ շարժվում».

  • F41. Open with it (gloss: "Why is the other line always faster? It is not. And airports, Disney and banks know exactly why you think so.").

Core idea. There is not one. There are seven: occupied time, four more Maister rules, inflated posted waits, the hidden queue, progress bars, the other line, the snake line. The card («մասամբ ժամացույցն է, մասամբ՝ զգացողությունը») is vague and the takeaway gives three instructions. This is two shorts: (a) how queue designers bend felt time, (b) why the other line seems faster. (b) is the channel's core material (probability plus what memory keeps) and already has a simulation.

Pacing.

  • F42. Beats 2-3 spend 32 s, a fifth of the video, on a story the video then calls unconfirmed, and beat 3 deflates the opening. Keep «Պատմում են», put «չստուգված պատմություն» on screen as a stamp (as the pilot did with «նկարազարդում»), and cut beat 3 to one clause.
  • F43. Cut beat 5 (rules): four sentences of the same shape ("X waiting is longer than Y") read by a neutral voice, the flattest 17 s in the batch. The two rules that matter are shown later anyway: unknown against known (progress bars), unfair against fair (snake line). Name each rule where it is used.
  • Beat 4: the last sentence (people overestimate their waits) floats; cut it.
  • F44. Cut beat 11 (card), and end beat 12 on «Միջինում այն քոնից արագ չէ» alone, without the three instructions.
  • F45. Structure, if it stays one video (about 2:10): other-line hook -> 1 in 3 and memory -> "queue designers know more tricks": Disney's posted times, the hidden queue, progress bars (Houston as a 6 s «Պատմում են» aside, or cut) -> the snake line, which fixes fairness and envy at once -> close on the other line. Then the hook and the ending loop; now they do not (7 against 6 minutes at the start, the next line at the end).
  • F46. Alternative: two shorts of 75-90 s each. Cleaner, but shorter than the length Hayk asked for.

Ending. Does not land: three instructions, then the one good line. No loop.

Visual ideas.

  • F47. The three-lines race as the opening shot: three columns of dots draining at random speeds, "you" in orange, 30 trips fast-forwarded in about 6 s with a tally. The 20 losses flash brighter (what memory keeps).
  • F48. Reveal the Disney queue: start at eye level (8 people and a wall), then lift the camera to a top view where 200 dots are folded behind the walls. The planned static top view gives the surprise away.

Armenian (questions).

  • F49 [heard] «զբաղված ժամանակ» / «դատարկ ժամանակ» (also «ժամանակդ զբաղված լինի», «զբաղեցրու ժամանակդ»): does «զբաղված» work for time and not a person? It carries rule 1, so it has to sound right.
  • F50 [heard] «իրականում սպասում ես ցուցադրվածի մոտ երկու երրորդը» is hard by ear («ցուցադրվածի մոտ» can be heard as "near the displayed"). Concrete numbers that match the picture (gloss: "the sign says 60 minutes, you wait about 40")?
  • F51 [heard] Beat 7 is one sentence with four clauses; split it.
  • F52 [ear] «ներբեռնման շերտը» for a progress bar (is that what people call it?); «Այն ուղևորներին վեց անգամ ավելի երկար քայլեցրեց»; «Սակայն դրա հետևում եղած գաղափարը լավ ուսումնասիրված է» (written register).

Verdict: needs rework (F41, F43, and F45 or F46).

5. crypto_paint

Showcase. 14 beats, 2,291 characters, about 2:41 (about 2:47 with numbers spoken: at the cap). The paint needs silent seconds, so one beat must go, better two.

11. Cross-cutting

Patterns that repeat

  1. One template. Hook question -> name and year of a study -> demonstration -> «Նույնը ...» and a second example -> an application -> a summary card -> «Այնպես որ, ...». It is clearest in bayes_test, simpson, regression, waiting, friendship and own_voice. Watched in a row, the viewer can predict the next beat by the third video.
  2. «Այնպես որ,» is in the closing of all ten, and of the pilot: it opens the last beat in eight, the second-to-last in friendship, and beat 9 in yawning. Four of the ten (bayes_test, simpson, regression, petrov) and the pilot end on the same sentence frame, «երբ ..., հարցրու՝ իսկ ...՞». As a deliberate signature for the statistics shorts ("the question to ask") it can work; then keep it for those and vary the lead-in.
  3. Double endings. Six of ten have a separate summary beat just before the close: «Կարճ ասած՝» in regression, waiting and own_voice, «Մաթեմատիկան պարզ է՝» in friendship, «Ինչ գիտենք՝» in yawning, the formula in bayes_test. Each card repeats an earlier beat or the takeaway itself. Rule: one closing beat, and the card is a picture under it. Saves 5-11 s a script.
  4. A long tail after the payoff. The main reveal lands at about 0:32 in own_voice, 0:54 in friendship, 0:59 in bayes_test, 1:02 in petrov, 1:11 in regression, 1:12 in simpson, 1:15 in crypto_paint, and 70-95 s of extensions follow. Waiting, qwerty and yawning have no single reveal. Each beat after the payoff should open with a new question (some do: «Ի՞նչ անել», «Իսկ ինչո՞ւ հաղթեց ...», «Արդյո՞ք նա միայնակ փրկեց աշխարհը») or go. One extension that surprises beats three that illustrate.
  5. Spoken years: 31 in the ten scripts (qwerty 7, petrov 5, yawning 4; simpson, crypto_paint and friendship 3 each). Each costs 2-2.5 s and sounds like a lecture. The source line on screen already has the author and the year. Speak a year only when the date is the point (1983; "secret until 1997").
  6. Bibliography as a beat, at 0:08-0:35, where retention is decided: «1985 թվականին Դեյվիդ Մայստերը ...», «1991 թվականին այն նկարագրեց սոցիոլոգ Սքոթ Ֆելդը ...», «1976 թվականին Ուիթֆիլդ Դիֆին և Մարտին Հելմանը ...», «Ճապոնացի հետազոտողներ Յասուոկաները ...». Names the viewer will not keep.
  7. Hooks. Strong: yawning, own_voice, regression, bayes_test, friendship (once the hedges move). Weakly built: petrov (date first, outcome given away), crypto_paint (a term first), simpson (a label first), qwerty (a myth the viewer may not know), waiting (a truism). The common fault: the first 2-4 s go to a date, a hedge, a label or a term. Six of ten first sentences run 4.5 s or longer.
  8. Honesty turning into the content. The story-or-data labelling is done properly everywhere (Houston, the legends, Petrov's role, the simulation in regression). The side effect: "they argue" / "disputed" / "nobody has proven" becomes the substance in qwerty (five times) and yawning (four). Honest doubt works after one solid thing; it does not work as the main course. Small gaps in the other direction: the friendship network and the own_voice frequency bars are invented illustrations with no «օրինակ» or «նկարազարդում» tag, and the covid bars are schematic drawings of real countries.
  9. No silence. The moments that need 1.5-3 s without voice: the bayes_test reveal, the simpson split, the regression reverse, the crypto_paint match, the petrov wait, own_voice's "try it", the yawning hook, the qwerty Morse dots. F122: an optional pause_after per beat in beats.hy.json, read by build_timeline.py and counted in the length budget.
  10. Numbers as digits change the length estimate and the caption timing (section 0). F123: count length with numbers spelled out, and let the timeline weight a digit token by its spoken length. (Both done since; see section 0, item 8.)

kids_tv against these patterns (section 12). It follows the template less closely than most and has no summary card. It does share four habits: «Այնպես որ,» opens its last beat too (now all eleven); two more spoken years (33 in all); a first sentence of 4.8 s; and pattern 8 in its second half, where beats 7-9 go claim, undo, claim, undo. It adds one pause to the list in pattern 9: the song loop in its beat 4.

Tone and consistency

  • F124. The ten are consistent with each other and drier than the pilot. The pilot talked («Մի շտապիր», «Հա, սովետական սառնարանները ...», «պապս ամբողջ կյանքում ծխել է»). These read: «Սակայն», «բազմիցս», «կիրառվում է», «ուստի», «հետագայում կասկածի տակ դրվեց», «այդ թվում՝». Is the drier register wanted, or should they be brought back towards the pilot?
  • F125. Local colour is almost gone. The pilot had grandmother's fridge and the Yerevan Zoo. In the ten scripts the Armenian touches are the names Ani and Aram, the phonetic keyboard (at 2:28 of qwerty) and the packed buses. Everything else is Berkeley, Houston, Disney, Harvard, Cincinnati. One local anchor per script would make them sound made here and not translated: a football coach sacked after a bad season (regression), a bank queue or an airport baggage belt in Yerevan (waiting), a marshrutka for the bus (friendship).
  • Address: «դու» everywhere (U-a), moving to «մենք» for general statements. Fine, except the crypto_paint hook (F61).
  • F126. «գրեթե» or «համարյա»: both are in use across the batch. One for the series?
  • F127 [ear] "The same with X": «Նույնն է ավտոբուսների հետ», «Նույնը դեմքի հետ է», «Նույնը շատ տեղերում է». The pilot had «Հին երգերի դեպքում էլ նույնն է». Is the «... հետ» form natural, or a calque?
  • F128 [ear] «Հետաքրքիրն այն է, որ» (crypto_paint, yawning) and «Եվ ամենահետաքրքիրը» (regression) tell the viewer that something is interesting. Cut the openers?
  • F129. TTS risks worth one cheap test call before voicing anything: acronyms («ԱՄՆ», «ԽՍՀՄ», «ՄԻԱՎ»), decades («1960-ականներին», «1860-70-ականներին»), digits with endings («99-ին», «69-ի», «26-ի»), Latin (QWERTY, Z, SE, TYPEWRITER).

The three strongest

  1. friendship. A personal claim, one mechanism shown in a picture that only a network can give, the best ending, the right length.
  2. petrov. The only real story, short beats, and the channel's own idea (weigh what is likely; do not obey the alarm) carried by a person. It needs re-cutting, not rewriting.
  3. own_voice. Universal, one idea, the shortest, the cheapest to make. With the audio demo (F106) it is the most shareable of the ten.

A close fourth is bayes_test: its first minute is the best explanation in the batch, and its second minute is four more lessons.

The two weakest

  1. qwerty. No idea a viewer can repeat, a hook built on a myth the viewer may not know, five "disputed", seven spoken years, over the length limit, and Latin letters the voice may mangle. It needs a spine before anything else.
  2. waiting. A truism for a hook, seven ideas, three takeaways, and a fifth of its time on a story it then calls unverified. The good short inside it is "why the other line is always faster".

kids_tv does not change either list. A clear answer by 1:19 and one good experiment put it above waiting and qwerty; an ordinary hook and a second half that takes back what it says keep it out of the top three. It sits in the middle of the batch, next to yawning.

Publishing order

F130. Suggested order (updated for kids_tv, which goes in at 7):

  1. own_voice: universal, simple visuals, a low-risk first upload after the pilot.
  2. bayes_test: the flagship statistics piece; it builds the crowd grid that simpson reuses.
  3. friendship: the first showcase; it builds the network that yawning reuses.
  4. petrov: a story, two videos after bayes_test, so a pointer to it (if kept) has something to point at.
  5. yawning: light, and its comment prompt helps a young channel.
  6. regression.
  7. kids_tv: a "why does X happen" piece between two heavier ones, with a ready audience in parents, and far enough from yawning that two "the evidence is mixed" videos do not run back to back.
  8. crypto_paint.
  9. simpson.
  10. waiting, after the rework.
  11. qwerty, after the rework.

The logic: never two "people as dots" statistics shorts in a row (bayes_test, regression and simpson sit at 2, 6 and 9); a story or a "why does X happen" piece between them; components are built before they are reused; the two reworks go last. If the showcases are animated first (Q16), start with friendship and petrov, and drop petrov's "in our Bayes video" line unless bayes_test is already out.
