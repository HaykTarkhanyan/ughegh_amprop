## The idea

The gossip hook (does your friend really know Ani's secret?), the same problem for websites and banks, then Where's
Waldo in one beat (a big sheet with a small hole shows him, not where he is), and a tease: the deck of cards, the two
balls and why it is called "zero knowledge" are in the long video. On YouTube, link the long video from the Short
(the "related video" field).
