# Armenian decisions

Decisions for the shorts series (03_shorts). They override the armenian-style-guide skill's defaults. Started on
2026-11-26 after the Codex review (X54): the same wording questions kept coming back in several plans, so they are
collected here once. Answers become rows in the table; the open list shrinks.

| ID | Decision | What it means in practice |
|----|----------|---------------------------|
| U1 | The narrator says «դու» to the viewer (Hayk, Q24, 2026-11-26) | Singular imperatives and possessives in every script («փորձիր», «հարցրու», «ընկերդ»); not a deviation to fix |
| U2 | Where's Waldo is «Վալոդ» (Hayk, Q25) | «Որտե՞ղ է Վալոդը» in the voice; our own character design on screen, the real book's art never shown |
| U3 | «փուլ», not «ռաունդ», for the rounds of a proof (Hayk, Q26) | «Քսան փուլից հետո», «Ամեն փուլում», the counter reads «Փուլ 10 / 20» |
| U4 | «գործնականում անհնար» for reversing the crypto maths (Hayk, Q27) | Arrow and closing of `crypto_paint`; the voice keeps «սովորական համակարգիչներով հայտնի չէ» as the qualifier |

## Open questions (waiting for Hayk)

Each question keeps its ID (O2, O3, ...) until it is answered; the U-ids in brackets are review points in the plans.

- **O2:** **«կարեկցանք» for two concepts.** In `yawning` it means the psychological construct *empathy*; in `dmanisi` it
   means *compassion / care*. Keep one word for both, or use a separate word for one of them (or the English term on
   screen at first mention)?
- **O3:** **«ռեգրեսիա դեպի միջինը»** for "regression to the mean" (`regression`, U-i). The glossary has only «գծային
   ռեգրեսիա».
- **O5:** **«զրոյական գիտելիքով ապացույց»** as the name of the idea (`zero_knowledge`, U-ppp).
- **O7:** **«քվերթի»** as the spoken form of QWERTY (`qwerty`, X55); the screen keeps «QWERTY».
- **O8:** **«ավելի ուժեղ ու թավ»** for the plugged-ears voice and «ավելի բարակ, պակաս թավ» for the recording (`own_voice`,
   X53): Codex's candidate wording.
- **O10:** **«սկրինինգ»** (`bayes_test`, U-b): now only on the source line, not in the voice; still OK on screen?
- **O11:** **«Միայն թե «ոչ»-ն էլ է տեղեկություն»** in the zero-knowledge gossip close: natural?
- **O12:** **How the voice reads «ճ»-ի / «ղ»-ի** in `mashtots` (letter names or sounds): check in the voice test before paying
    for the full script.

## Not errors (from the Codex review, so they do not come back as "fixes")

- «հանճարի խոսքերը» in the Mashtots quotation is established church wording; leave the Classical sentence as is.
- «բաց Գուգլ քարտեզները» was a valid imperative («բա՛ց»); the map script now says «միացրու գլոբուսի տեսքը» anyway.
- Singular address is the project's choice (U1), not an error to correct.
