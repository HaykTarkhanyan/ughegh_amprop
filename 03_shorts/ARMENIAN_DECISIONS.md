# Armenian decisions

Decisions for the shorts series (03_shorts). They override the armenian-style-guide skill's defaults. Started on
2026-11-26 after the Codex review (X54): the same wording questions kept coming back in several plans, so they are
collected here once. Answers become rows in the table; the open list shrinks.

## Open questions (waiting for Hayk)

Each question names the scripts it touches; the U-ids in brackets are the review points in those plans.

1. **Address: «դու» or «դուք».** Every script now says «դու» (imperatives «փորձիր», «հարցրու»). The style guide's
   default is plural for teaching text, but the series' conversational voice is a separate choice. All scripts (U-a).
2. **«կարեկցանք» for two concepts.** In `yawning` it means the psychological construct *empathy*; in `dmanisi` it
   means *compassion / care*. Keep one word for both, or use a separate word for one of them (or the English term on
   screen at first mention)?
3. **«ռեգրեսիա դեպի միջինը»** for "regression to the mean" (`regression`, U-i). The glossary has only «գծային
   ռեգրեսիա».
4. **«ռաունդ»** in `zero_knowledge` (U-ppp): natural in your mouth, or «փուլ»?
5. **«զրոյական գիտելիքով ապացույց»** as the name of the idea (`zero_knowledge`, U-ppp).
6. **«Ուոլդո» or «Ուոլի»** for Where's Waldo (`zero_knowledge`, `zero_knowledge_short`, U-sss).
7. **«քվերթի»** as the spoken form of QWERTY (`qwerty`, X55); the screen keeps «QWERTY».
8. **«ավելի ուժեղ ու թավ»** for the plugged-ears voice and «ավելի բարակ, պակաս թավ» for the recording (`own_voice`,
   X53): Codex's candidate wording.
9. **«գործնականում անհնար»** on the crypto arrow and in the closing (`crypto_paint`, X44); Codex suggested
   «գործնականում դժվար».
10. **«սկրինինգ»** (`bayes_test`, U-b): now only on the source line, not in the voice; still OK on screen?
11. **«Միայն թե «ոչ»-ն էլ է տեղեկություն»** in the zero-knowledge gossip close: natural?
12. **How the voice reads «ճ»-ի / «ղ»-ի** in `mashtots` (letter names or sounds): check in the voice test before paying
    for the full script.

## Not errors (from the Codex review, so they do not come back as "fixes")

- «հանճարի խոսքերը» in the Mashtots quotation is established church wording; leave the Classical sentence as is.
- «բաց Գուգլ քարտեզները» was a valid imperative («բա՛ց»); the map script now says «միացրու գլոբուսի տեսքը» anyway.
- Singular address is a project choice still open (question 1), not an error to correct silently.
