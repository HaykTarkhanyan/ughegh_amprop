# Short: Areni-1's oldest known surviving leather shoe - revised script and plan

Revised for Hayk's review, 2026-11-28. History strand with an Armenian subject. Script source: `beats.hy.json`.
One construction showcase; one preservation extension. No voice or animation generated.
K3 was read for context; the winery remains a separate subject.

## The idea in one paragraph

A leather shoe under a sheep-dung covering, still with its laces, is a stronger opening than an age alone.
Areni-1 supplies the local anchor inside the discovery. The showcase follows the single-piece right-foot wrap
through its four back and fifteen top eyelet pairs, using the real shoe photo alongside a tagged reconstruction.
Its 24.5 cm length and approximate EU size 37 give scale. Three leather measurements support the dating, and
a side-by-side timeline makes the age tangible: the Areni shoe predates Ötzi's footwear. The one record-category
doubt distinguishes the oldest known surviving leather shoe from older fibre sandals and the invention of shoes.
The single extension explains preservation through the cave conditions and covering deposit, then shows two
possible grass functions with just one spoken qualification. The close returns to the museum object and its
unknown owner.

## Facts (checked)

Evidence base: `_knowledge/short_videos/facts_codex_5_mixed_2026-11-28.md`, K2.
Each source below is from the sheet. **NEW** identifies evidence newly used in this revision.

- **Location and discovery year - high** (`find`): Areni-1, Vayots Dzor, Armenia; discovered 2008, with year on
  the source line. [History Museum of Armenia, catalogue](https://historymuseum.am/collections_type/5500-%D5%A1%D5%B4%D5%B5%D5%A1-%D5%AF%D5%A1%D5%B7%D5%BE%D5%A5-%D5%B8%D5%BF%D5%B6%D5%A1%D5%B4%D5%A1%D5%B6/).
- **Age and calibrated interval - high** (`hook`, `dating_comparison`): about 5,500 years; combined leather range
  3627-3377 calibrated BCE from Table 2. This is not a precise birthday or raw BP age.
  [Pinhasi et al., 2010, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/).
- **NEW: three leather measurements - high** (`dating_comparison`): Table 1 lists three measurements of leather,
  two Oxford and one Irvine. No uncalibrated BP values are read as calendar years.
  [Pinhasi et al., Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/).
- **One-piece wrap, surviving laces and right foot - high** (`hook`, `find`, `construction`): one leather piece,
  with surviving leather lacing and wear/shape consistent with the right foot. It is not a modern separately
  attached rigid-sole shoe. [Pinhasi et al., Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/),
  [UCLA, 2010](https://international.ucla.edu/apc/article/115597).
- **NEW: eyelet pairs - high** (`construction`): four back sets/pairs and fifteen across the top. These are pairs,
  not fifteen individual holes. The reconstruction follows the paper's photograph rather than inventing a cutting
  pattern. [Pinhasi et al., Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/).
- **Dimensions - high** (`construction`): 24.5 cm, approximately EU size 37; does not determine the wearer's sex.
  [Pinhasi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/),
  [UCLA](https://international.ucla.edu/apc/article/115597).
- **NEW: older than Ötzi's footwear - high** (`dating_comparison`): the Areni leather interval precedes the
  approximate 3365-3118 calibrated BCE date for Ötzi given in the paper's Discussion. The paper's table differs
  by one year at the older endpoint; use the stated approximate Discussion interval consistently. Ötzi's name and
  dates are on screen; no claim about his location, death or shoe construction is added.
  [Pinhasi et al., Discussion and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/).
- **Leather record and older fibre sandals - high** (`find`, `doubt`): oldest known surviving leather shoe;
  older plant-fibre sandals exist. This does not establish where footwear was invented.
  [Pinhasi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/),
  [Guinness, First leather shoe](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/first-leather-shoe).
- **Covering deposit and cave conditions - high for observations; medium for individual causal contributions**
  (`hook`, `preservation`): stable, cool, dry conditions and thick sheep dung covering the deposits.
  Researchers credit the conditions together; the voice attributes this explanation once, without assigning

- **Grass - high for presence; medium for purpose** (`grass`): grass inside; insulation or retaining shape/storage
  are possibilities. The paper favours shape/storage, without establishing either purpose directly.
  [Pinhasi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/),
  [UCLA](https://international.ucla.edu/apc/article/115597).
- **Museum and unidentified owner - high** (`closing`): museum collection is documented, but no individual owner
  is identified. No claim about a particular gallery's present opening or the wearer's ethnicity/sex.
  [Museum catalogue](https://historymuseum.am/collections_type/5500-%D5%A1%D5%B4%D5%B5%D5%A1-%D5%AF%D5%A1%D5%B7%D5%BE%D5%A5-%D5%B8%D5%BF%D5%B6%D5%A1%D5%B4%D5%A1%D5%B6/),
  [Pinhasi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2882957/).
