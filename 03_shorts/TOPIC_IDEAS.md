## Strange but true history
- The Great Emu War: in 1932 the Australian army went after emus with machine guns, and the emus won
- The Dancing Plague of 1518: a crowd in Strasbourg danced for days, and nobody fully knows why
- The Boston Molasses Flood: in 1919 a wave of molasses ran down a city street and killed 21 people
- The shortest war in history: Britain vs Zanzibar in 1896, over in under an hour
- Tsutomu Yamaguchi survived both Hiroshima and Nagasaki
- Stanislav Petrov: the Soviet officer who treated a 1983 nuclear alarm as false, and was right (folder: `petrov/`)
- A driver's wrong turn put Franz Ferdinand right in front of his assassin
- 1816, the year without a summer: a volcano caused famine across the Northern Hemisphere, and the gloomy summer
  gave us Frankenstein

## Time perspective (these make good short videos)
- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid
- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire

## History myths people still believe
- Vikings didn't wear horned helmets. That image comes mostly from 19th-century art and opera costumes
- Medieval people knew the Earth was round
- Napoleon was about average height for his time
- The Library of Alexandria wasn't destroyed in one big fire. It declined over centuries
- Semmelweis told doctors to wash their hands, other doctors mocked him, and he died in an asylum

## Art
- The Mona Lisa only became the world's most famous painting after it was stolen in 1911
- Greek statues were originally painted in bright colors (folder: `painted_statues/`)
- Ultramarine blue was ground from lapis lazuli and could cost more than gold, which is why painters saved it for the
  Virgin Mary's robe
- "Mummy brown": painters really did use paint made from ground-up Egyptian mummies
- The forger who sold a fake Vermeer to Goering, then had to prove it was fake to avoid a treason charge
- Why babies in medieval paintings look like tiny old men
- How Brunelleschi's mirror experiment brought perspective into Renaissance painting
- Banksy's painting that shredded itself right after selling at auction
- Did Vermeer use lenses or mirrors to paint? Art historians still argue about it
