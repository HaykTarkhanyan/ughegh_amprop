Open questions (waiting for Hayk)

Each question keeps its ID (O2, O3, ...) until it is answered; the U-ids in brackets are review points in the plans.

  • O2: «կարեկցանք» for two concepts. In yawning it means the psychological construct empathy; in dmanisi it means compassion / care. Keep one word for both, or use a separate word for one of them (or the English term on screen at first mention)?
  • O3: «ռեգրեսիա դեպի միջինը» for "regression to the mean" (regression, U-i). The glossary has only «գծային ռեգրեսիա».
  • O5: «զրոյական գիտելիքով ապացույց» as the name of the idea (zero_knowledge, U-ppp).
  • O7: «քվերթի» as the spoken form of QWERTY (qwerty, X55); the screen keeps «QWERTY».
  • O8: «ավելի ուժեղ ու թավ» for the plugged-ears voice and «ավելի բարակ, պակաս թավ» for the recording (own_voice, X53): Codex's candidate wording.
  • O10: «սկրինինգ» (bayes_test, U-b): now only on the source line, not in the voice; still OK on screen?
  • O11: «Միայն թե «ոչ»-ն էլ է տեղեկություն» in the zero-knowledge gossip close: natural?
  • O12: How the voice reads «ճ»-ի / «ղ»-ի in mashtots (letter names or sounds): check in the voice test before paying for the full script.
